Judith's Beach Day

Roll Judith's wheelchair across the sand, nip ankles near the water for bigger points, and dig trenches to trap the lifeguards.

Arrow keys or WASD to roll · Space to dig · P to pause · on a phone, drag to roll

Who's on the beach

SunbatherAsleep on a towel. Easy points.10
WalkerBolts once Judith rolls close.20
SurferHeading for the waves. Get them before they paddle out.25
JoggerFast, and always on the wet sand.40
Trapped lifeguardStuck in one of Judith's trenches.60

Points are multiplied by how close the person is to the water: x3 at the shoreline down to x1 by the boardwalk. The scale is marked on the left edge of the sand.

Rolling conditions

Wet sandPacked firm by the tide. Fastest.
BoardwalkSmooth planks along the bottom.
Dry sandSoft. The wheels sink in.
Shallow waterShe can wade in, barely.

House rules

  • Combo: bite again within 2.6 seconds to raise the multiplier, up to x5.
  • Lifeguards: a couple of quick bites brings the first one down from the tower, and more bring his backup. Getting caught stuns Judith, resets the combo and costs 3 seconds.
  • Trenches: press Space (or Dig) to dig a hole in front of Judith. A lifeguard who runs into it is stuck for a few seconds: +30, and he's worth 60 if she bites him while he's down. Up to 3 open holes at a time.
  • Tennis ball: grab it for 5 seconds of zoomies.
